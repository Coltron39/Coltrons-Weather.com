## Before you go live

1. Start `wx_feed.py` and wait for the health light in the control panel to go green.
   Your numbers are stale until it does — the times above are from the 8 PM hour.
2. Open `PROGRAM` and confirm `desk.html` is above the camera and the headline reads
   "FIRST ALERT NIGHTCAST — starts at 9 PM". That's the pre-show card, and it flips to
   the first headline at 9:00 on its own.
3. Check the ticker is running the tonight/Tuesday copy, not "LOADING".
4. Rehearse any moment of the show by opening `desk.html?now=21:13` in a browser.

## If the weather changes on you

The one thing that would rewrite this show is a watch or warning going up. The control
panel cuts to your alert scene automatically when a new one appears, and the ticker
picks it up without you touching anything. If that happens, drop the seven-day segment,
not the storm timing.
